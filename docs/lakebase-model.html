finance From files to balances and receipt lines

Folders of statement PDFs, CSV exports and receipts are the source of truth. Each file is registered once, its balances become points in time, its rows become bank transactions stored once however many files show them, and receipts tie their lines to the charges they explain.

Cross-domain links

  1. Calendar date joins everything. core.daily_summary shows, per day: journal entry, habits, workout minutes, calories, beers, weight, spending, income, garden observations and posts.
  2. Journal and health share meals and workouts. The journal page and the Health tracker write to the same journal.meals and journal.workouts; the health views read them.
  3. Journal account lists are balance snapshots. Each entry's list of account balances becomes finance.balance_snapshots rows (source journal) dated to the entry, linked to an account once it is identified.
  4. Purchases link to nutrition. An item catalog entry can reference a health.foods row, so purchases and meals share a food identity.
  5. Savings goals link to the pay plan. A goal references the allocation that funds it each paycheck; its balances are snapshots over time.

Business rules the model enforces

journalhealth

  1. One journal entry per calendar date; moving an entry carries its tags, habits and meals.
  2. Many weigh-ins per date; the trend uses morning readings, others stay as references.
  3. Planned meals are never counted as eaten; calories include alcohol.
  4. Dog walks are recorded but never qualify a workout day.
  5. Goal weight derives from the BMI goal and height; the profile keeps its history.

finance

  1. Amounts keep the bank's sign: positive leaves the account, negative arrives.
  2. Each file is registered once by content hash; a transaction is stored once however many files show it, found again by bank id or fingerprint.
  3. A matched receipt explains its bank charge and is never counted twice; a receipt can be split across tenders; its lines equal its net and net + tax + tip equals its total.
  4. For credit cards and loans the balance is the amount owed and charges raise it; for deposit accounts charges lower it.
  5. Statement opening and closing balances must come from a statement.
  6. Movements (pay, transfers, card payments) are never spending; every movement has a type.
  7. A category belongs to at most one budget; a budget can pool several.
  8. Balances are observations over time; the latest wins.

gardenmusiccontent

  1. One active season per bed; each bed's shape carries the dimensions its type needs.
  2. A song is separate from its recordings; drafts never appear in the public catalog.
  3. Only published posts appear publicly; messages are private.

all

  1. Every writer owns its rows by source and replaces only those.
  2. Unknown is not zero: missing values stay empty.
  3. Copied rows keep the source's original record in details.
  4. Single owner: no user_id yet.

Open questions

  1. Account identities. Statements, receipts and paycheck deposits name accounts only by institution and last four digits, and some cards appear only on receipts. Each needs an institution, a name and a type.
  2. Retirement account institutions for the retirement balances.
  3. Journal account lists sometimes name an institution without an account.
  4. Music links. One catalog song points to an audio file that isn't in static/audio, and another points to the root folder while its file is in an album folder.
  5. Discretionary pool membership is read from the budget's note: Shopping, Dining, Entertainment, Personal, Health, Professional, Home, Fees, Review.
  6. Statement files. The tracker names the statements its rows came from but not their periods or balances. A sample CSV and statement from each institution is needed to write their import profiles.
